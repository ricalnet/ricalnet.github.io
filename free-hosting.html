FAQ

Quick answers to common questions

Who qualifies for free hosting?

Students of all levels in Indonesia — from high school to university. Your project must be Docker‑based and open‑source.

How long can I host my project for free?

The standard retention period is 1 month. If your project is active and you'd like to keep it live longer, just reach out — RICALNET is flexible for students who are actively working on their projects.

What kind of projects can I host?

Anything that runs in Docker! Portfolios, personal blogs, web applications, API services, final year projects, open‑source tools, assignment demos, and more. The only restriction: your project must be open‑source and not used for illegal activities.

Do I get a custom domain?

You get a subdomain under *.ricalnet.my.id — e.g., myproject.ricalnet.my.id. If you have your own domain, RICALNET can help you point it to your project as well.

Is my data safe and private?

Infrastructure security is provided by RICALNET. This includes automatic TLS encryption. However, application‑level security — such as input validation, authentication logic, and data handling — is the responsibility of the student developer. RICALNET advises following secure coding practices.

Still have questions? Reach out to RICALNET — the team is here to help.
