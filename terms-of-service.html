Introduction

Welcome to RICALNET. These Terms of Service govern your use of RICALNET's infrastructure, tools, and services. By using any RICALNET service, you agree to these terms.

RICALNET is an open-source self-hosted infrastructure platform designed to help you escape vendor lock-in, regain digital sovereignty, and maintain privacy. RICALNET believes in transparency, fairness, and the right to control your own data.

Service Availability

Exclusive Services (SSO, Encrypted Chat, Cloud Storage & Password Manager): These services are currently available exclusively to activists and journalists — individuals who face elevated digital threats. This prioritization reflects our core mission to protect those most at risk.

If you believe your work requires similar protections under extraordinary circumstances, please contact us to discuss eligibility.

Free and with long-term commitment: RICALNET provides these services at no cost to activists and journalists, and we are deeply committed to their indefinite availability — subject to available operational resources. Should RICALNET ever face a critical resource shortfall or structural change requiring service modification, we will provide at least 90 days' advance notice and offer migration assistance.

For activists and journalists, RICALNET guarantees that all data is handled with the strictest confidentiality, in full alignment with the Privacy Policy. No data is shared, accessed, or disclosed to any third party under any circumstances, unless explicitly instructed by the user.

If you are an activist or journalist seeking secure communication and sovereign infrastructure, reach out to RICALNET and you'll get onboarded.

Priority access: These services are RICALNET's top priority. Even as resources fluctuate, activists and journalists will always have first claim on available capacity.

3

Free Hosting for Students

RICALNET offers free hosting for students of all levels in Indonesia who are building open-source projects. RICALNET believes in nurturing the next generation of developers and contributors.

To qualify, your project must be open-source and published under a recognized open-source license (MIT, GPL, Apache, or similar). The source code must be publicly accessible on platforms like GitHub, GitLab, or similar.

Docker-only: RICALNET accepts only Docker-based projects for free hosting. Docker ensures portability, isolation, and ease of management — making it the ideal format for RICALNET's lightweight infrastructure. Projects must provide a docker-compose.yml or equivalent Dockerfile that can be deployed with a single command.

Retention Period: Hosting is provided for a 1-month retention period. This means your project will remain hosted for a minimum of one month from the date of deployment.

Explicit Notice & Takedown Procedure: Before any data removal at the end of the retention period or due to inactivity, RICALNET will:

  • Send a 7-day advance notice via email;
  • Make at least three reasonable attempts to contact you; and
  • Retain archived data for an additional 30 days post-removal in case we re-establish contact.

Why open source? RICALNET believes in giving back. Your project's source code becomes a resource for others to learn from, fork, and build upon. That's how the open-source ecosystem thrives.

To apply for free hosting, send your project details to ricalnet@duck.com with the subject "Student Hosting Application".

4

Open Source Contribution Requirement

As a condition of receiving free hosting, your project's source code must remain publicly available and open-source for the duration of the hosting period and beyond. This ensures that the code can be studied, modified, and used by others — advancing the principles of transparency and collaboration that RICALNET stands for.

RICALNET does not require any form of exclusivity or ownership over your code. You retain full rights to your project. All RICALNET asks is that you keep it open and accessible, so others can benefit from your work.

What RICALNET doesn't require: No requirement to contribute to RICALNET specifically. Your project can be anything — a web app, a CLI tool, a library, a game, or any other open-source work. The open-source ethos is what matters.

Data Retention & Privacy

1-Month Retention: As mentioned, free hosting is offered with a 1-month retention period. This means your project's data and hosted files will be retained for a minimum of one month. After this period, RICALNET may contact you to confirm if you wish to continue. If no response is received, or if resources become critically constrained, RICALNET may archive or remove your data following the explicit notice procedure described in Section 3.

Privacy Commitment: Despite limited resources, RICALNET applies enterprise-grade security and privacy practices across all services. This includes:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Zero-trust access control with SSO/OIDC authentication
  • No third-party analytics or tracking
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Strict data access policies — your data is yours alone

For full details, please refer to the Privacy Policy.
