Introduction

Service Availability

Exclusive services (SSO, encrypted chat, cloud storage, password manager): Available only to activists and journalists — people facing elevated digital threats. This is our core mission: protect those most at risk.

If you believe your work needs similar protection, contact us to discuss eligibility.

Free and committed for the long term: RICALNET provides these services at no cost to activists and journalists. We are deeply committed to keeping them available — subject to operational resources. If a critical resource shortfall or structural change ever requires service modification, we will give at least 90 days' notice and offer migration assistance.

For activists and journalists, all data is handled with the strictest confidentiality, fully aligned with our Privacy Policy. No data is shared, accessed, or disclosed to any third party unless you explicitly instruct us.

If you are an activist or journalist seeking secure communication and sovereign infrastructure, reach out and we'll get you onboarded.

Priority access: These services are our top priority. Even as resources fluctuate, activists and journalists always have first claim on available capacity.

3

Free Hosting for Students

RICALNET offers free hosting for students of all levels in Indonesia who build open-source projects. We believe in nurturing the next generation of developers.

To qualify, your project must be open-source under a recognized license (MIT, GPL, Apache, or similar) and publicly accessible on GitHub, GitLab, or similar platforms.

Podman-only: We accept only Podman-based projects. Podman ensures portability, isolation, and ease of management — ideal for our lightweight infrastructure. Your project must include a podman-compose.yml or equivalent Containerfile deployable with a single command.

Retention: Hosting runs for a 1-month retention period, renewable. Your project stays hosted for a minimum of one month from deployment.

Notice & Takedown: Before any data removal, RICALNET will:

  • Send a 7-day advance notice via email
  • Make at least three attempts to contact you
  • Retain archived data for an additional 30 days in case we re-establish contact

Why open source? Because your code becomes a resource for others to learn from, fork, and build upon. That's how the ecosystem thrives.

Apply by sending your project details to ricalnet@duck.com with subject "Student Hosting Application".

Open Source Requirement

As a condition of free hosting, your project's source code must stay public and open-source for the hosting period and beyond. This ensures others can study, modify, and use your work — advancing the transparency and collaboration we stand for.

RICALNET claims no exclusivity or ownership over your code. You keep full rights to your project. All we ask: keep it open and accessible so others can benefit.

No strings attached: You don't need to contribute to RICALNET specifically. Your project can be anything — a web app, a CLI tool, a library, a game. The open-source ethos is what matters.

Data Retention & Privacy

1-month retention: Free hosting is offered with a 1-month retention period. Your project's data stays for at least one month. After that, RICALNET may contact you to confirm continuation. If no response, or if resources become critically constrained, we may archive or remove your data following the notice procedure in Section 3.

Privacy commitment: Despite limited resources, RICALNET applies enterprise-grade security and privacy practices across all services:

  • End-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest
  • Zero-trust access control with SSO/OIDC authentication
  • No third-party analytics or tracking
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Strict data access policies — your data is yours alone
