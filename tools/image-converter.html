FAQ

Common questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. All conversion happens entirely in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your images never leave your device — no uploads, no servers, no logs. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

Which format should I choose?

Use WebP or AVIF for the best balance of quality and file size on the web. Choose PNG when you need transparency or lossless compression. JPG offers universal compatibility.

Can I convert multiple images at once?

Yes. Select multiple files at once — the tool handles batch conversion and lets you download each file individually or all at once.

Are there file size limits?

The only practical limit is your device's available memory. Very large images (100+ MB) may be slow or fail on low-memory devices. For best results, keep individual files under 20 MB.
