How It Works

Draw, refine, export

Everything runs in your browser. No sign-up, no uploads, no tracking.

1. Draw

Pick a tool, color, and brush size. Draw freehand with the pen, or add shapes for structured diagrams.

2. Refine

Undo and redo any action. Use the eraser for small fixes, or clear the canvas to start fresh. Keyboard shortcuts make it fast.

3. Export

Download your canvas as PNG (lossless) or JPG (smaller file). Perfect for sharing, docs, and design work.

FAQ

Common questions

Is my drawing saved or uploaded anywhere?

No. Your drawing exists only in your browser's memory — it's never uploaded, stored, or tracked. Close the tab and it's gone. Use the export buttons to save your work as PNG or JPG before you leave.

Can I use keyboard shortcuts?

Yes. P for Pen, E for Eraser, L for Line, R for Rectangle, O for Circle, C to Clear. Ctrl+Z for Undo, Ctrl+Y for Redo. Shortcuts work anywhere on the page — no need to click first.

Does it work on mobile or tablet?

Yes. The canvas supports touch and stylus input on modern browsers. For the best experience, use a tablet with a stylus or a desktop with a mouse — precise drawing is easier with a pointer.

What file formats can I export?

Two formats: PNG for lossless, sharp output — ideal for diagrams, wireframes, and screenshots. JPG for smaller file sizes — good for photos and quick shares where perfect quality isn't critical.
