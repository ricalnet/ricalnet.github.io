FAQ

Common questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All processing runs entirely in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your image never leaves your device — no uploads, no servers, no logs. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads.

What's the difference between pixelate, blur, and solid?

Pixelate averages blocks of pixels for a mosaic effect. Blur applies a Gaussian blur for a soft, less obvious redaction. Solid fills the region with an opaque color — the most secure option, since no original data remains underneath.

Can I undo a redaction?

Yes — the tool keeps a history of every edit. Click Undo Last to step back, or Reset to restore the original image. History is stored in memory only and disappears when you close the page.

What image formats are supported?

The tool accepts any image format your browser can render: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, SVG, AVIF. Export is available in PNG (lossless), JPG, and WebP. Maximum file size is 20 MB.
