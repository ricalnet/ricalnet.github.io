FAQ

Essential answers

Everything you need to know about digital rights, sovereign infrastructure, and private services.

What does "digital sovereignty" mean in practice?

Digital sovereignty means you own and control your infrastructure and data. We deploy self-hosted, open-source systems with no proprietary APIs, no closed ecosystems, and no data extraction. Your stack is portable, modular, and fully under your authority — not a vendor's.

How do you protect digital rights?

Zero-trust architecture, sovereign identity management, threat monitoring, intrusion detection, and incident response — all delivered through self-hosted infrastructure you control. No third-party surveillance, no data commodification.

What private services do you offer?

We provide rights-respecting digital tools including encrypted chat, sovereign cloud storage, password manager, self-hosted AI assistant, private photo gallery, no-tracking search engine, translation, and URL shortener. All accessible via secure private networks without surveillance.

Are the private services truly free?

Yes. No fees, no limits, no data collection, no surveillance. Funded by organizations that value digital rights and sovereign infrastructure.

How do I get started with sovereign infrastructure?

Contact us for a consultation. We'll assess your current infrastructure, identify rights risks, and design sovereign systems that put you back in control.
