Privacy Commitment

Transparency · Data sovereignty · No compromise.

No tracking No analytics Your data, your control
1

Data We Collect

No personal data from visitors. No tracking cookies. No third-party analytics. The web server stores standard logs (IP, time, pages) only for security and maintenance — rotated periodically, never used for behavior analysis.

For services that require accounts, user-provided data (username, encrypted password, files) stays on our servers. You keep full control.

2

How We Use Data

Technical logs are used only for infrastructure stability and security. Account data and uploaded content stay under your control. We do not process your data beyond basic operations.

3

Data Sovereignty

We do not open, access, or disclose your data to anyone — including government agencies or law enforcement — except on your direct written instruction.

Servers are under our physical and technical control. No backdoors. No forced access. No cooperation with external data requests.

If faced with a binding legal request, we will resist by all available means — including legal assistance and public disclosure.

Your data belongs entirely to you.

4

Security

We apply industry-standard security practices: regular updates and hardened server configs. Still, no system is 100% safe. Keep your credentials confidential.

5

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. For account services, do this directly from each service's control panel. If you hit technical issues, contact our support.
