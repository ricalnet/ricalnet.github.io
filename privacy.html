Privacy Commitment

Full transparency · Data sovereignty · No compromise

No tracking No third-party analytics Your data, your control
1

Data Collected

This site does not collect personal data from visitors. There are no tracking cookies or third-party analytics services. The web server stores standard log records (IP address, access time, requested pages) solely for security and system maintenance purposes. These logs are stored temporarily, rotated periodically, and not used for user behavior analysis.

For services requiring account registration user-provided data (username, encrypted password, files) is stored on the provided servers. Users retain full control over their data.

2

Data Usage

Technical log data is used only to ensure service infrastructure stability and security. Account data and content uploaded to private services remain entirely under user control. The service operator does not process this data for any purpose beyond basic operational service functions.

3

Data Sovereignty Commitment

The operator will not open, access, or disclose user data to any party— including government agencies, law enforcement, or other authorities—under any circumstances, except upon direct written instruction from the user concerned.

Servers are under the physical and technical control of the operator, and are not equipped with backdoor mechanisms, forced access, or voluntary cooperation with external data requests.

In the event of a binding legal request, the operator will resist by all available means, including but not limited to legal assistance and public disclosure of information.

User data belongs entirely to the user.

4

Security

Industry-standard security practices are applied to protect communication, including regular software updates and tightened server configurations. Nevertheless, it should be understood that no system is completely free from security risks. Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential.

5

User Rights

Users have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data stored in the services. For service accounts, these actions can be performed independently through each service's control panel. If technical issues arise, users can contact the available support channels.
