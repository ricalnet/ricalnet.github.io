Mission & Vision

Why we exist

Our mission is to build sovereign digital infrastructure that protects rights. Our vision is a free and open internet where every individual has control.

Mission

To build and maintain sovereign, self-hosted infrastructure that protects digital rights, eliminates vendor lock-in, and ensures complete data sovereignty for individuals and organizations.

Sovereign Open Source Rights-First

Vision

A world where digital rights are inalienable — where every person and organization can participate in the digital economy without sacrificing privacy, autonomy, or freedom.

Privacy Freedom Autonomy
Our Framework

3 Pillars of Digital Independence

The foundational principles that guide everything we build.

Pillar 1

Data Sovereignty

You own your data — completely. No proprietary ecosystems, no vendor lock-in, no third-party access. Your infrastructure, your keys, your rules.

Self-Hosted Zero Lock-in
Pillar 2

Identity & Access Sovereignty

Your identity is yours to control. Unified, secure access across all your services — OIDC, SAML, LDAP — with you as the ultimate authority.

SSO Zero-Trust
Pillar 3

Operational Sovereignty

You control how your infrastructure operates. Continuous monitoring, threat detection, incident response — all managed on your terms, without external dependency.

Self-Managed Transparent

These three pillars form the foundation of every infrastructure we build. No compromise. No exceptions.
