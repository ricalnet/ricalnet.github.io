The Framework

3 Pillars of RICALNET

Control. Access. Libre. No compromises.

Pillar 1

Control

You own your data. Self‑hosted, no proprietary APIs, no third‑party access. Your keys, your rules.

Self‑Hosted Zero Lock‑in
Pillar 2

Access

Never trust, always verify. Strict auth (OIDC/SAML/LDAP), encryption, and monitoring — no external identity providers. Secure access for all.

SSO Zero‑Trust
Pillar 3

Libre

Pay only for what you use. No per‑seat licenses, no egress fees, no surprise bills. Predictable costs that scale.

Self‑Managed Transparent

Every resource — from the public repo to the in‑house tools — embodies these pillars. Control. Access. Libre. No compromises.
