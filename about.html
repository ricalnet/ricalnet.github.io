About RICALNET — Open Source Self-Hosted Infrastructure

RICALNET is a Robust Infrastructure for Control, Access, & Libre NETwork. RICALNET exists to give you the tools to escape vendor lock‑in, cut costs, and run zero‑trust infrastructure — whether you're a company, an activist, or a homelab enthusiast.

Mission & Vision

Why RICALNET exists

To build a Robust Infrastructure for Control, Access, & Libre NETwork — open‑source, self‑hosted, and free from vendor lock‑in.

Mission

To provide a comprehensive suite of open‑source tools, configurations, and resources — including a public repository, in‑house utilities, and documentation — so anyone can build sovereign, cost‑effective infrastructure without proprietary traps. Control. Access. Libre.

Open Source Zero‑Trust No Lock‑in

Vision

A world where sovereignty is the default. Where every organization and individual can freely choose and run privacy‑respecting, open‑source services — without surveillance, without exorbitant costs, and without being locked into any vendor. Freedom, built in.

Privacy Freedom Autonomy
The Framework

3 Pillars of RICALNET

The foundational principles that guide RICALNET.

Pillar 1

Control

You own your data. The resources ensure your infrastructure is self‑hosted, with no proprietary APIs or third‑party access. Your keys, your rules. Full sovereignty.

Self‑Hosted Zero Lock‑in
Pillar 2

Access

Never trust, always verify. The configurations enforce strict authentication (OIDC/SAML/LDAP), encryption, and continuous monitoring — all without relying on external identity providers. Secure access for all.

SSO Zero‑Trust
Pillar 3

Libre

Pay only for what you use. The open‑source stack eliminates per‑seat licenses, egress fees, and surprise bills. Predictable costs that scale with your needs. Free as in freedom.

Self‑Managed Transparent

Every resource — from the public repo to the in‑house tools — embodies these pillars. Control. Access. Libre. No compromises.

RICALNET's Values

What drives RICALNET

These are the principles that shape culture, products, and projects.

Privacy First

Open Source

No Lock‑in

Digital Freedom
